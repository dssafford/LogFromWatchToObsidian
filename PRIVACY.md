# Privacy Policy

This is a personal, non-commercial application used by a single individual
(the repository owner) to sync their **own** health data from the Oura API
into their **own** private Obsidian notes.

- No data is collected from any other person.
- No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
- All data is stored locally on the owners personal computer.
- OAuth access tokens are stored locally and used solely to read the
  owners own Oura data.

For questions, open an issue on this repository.
